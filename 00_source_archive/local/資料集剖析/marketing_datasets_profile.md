### 2.7 RFM 驗證（任務指定）

#### 定義

**【評註】本檔採用的 RFM 定義（依驗證結果反推確認）：**

$$

$$

$$

### 2.8 【材料原文】CAI 方法論完整還原 — 這回答什麼行銷/商業問題？

> **回答的問題：**
> 「這位客戶跟**他自己的過去**相比，最近是**變得更常刷卡**，還是**正在悄悄流失**？」
>
> RFM 的 R 只告訴你「多久沒來」，**無法區分**「本來就一年來一次的人剛好 300 天沒來（正常）」
> 和「本來每週來的人 30 天沒來（警訊）」。
> CAI 用**每位客戶自己的歷史消費節奏當基準**，解決了 R 的跨客戶不可比問題。
> 這是**流失預警（churn early-warning）**與**再行銷時機（re-engagement timing）**的核心指標。

#### 數學定義

設客戶 $i$ 的消費日序列為 $d_{i,1} < d_{i,2} < \cdots < d_{i,n_i}$（**同日多筆已合併為一天**），
相鄰間隔為：

$$
g_{i,k} = d_{i,k+1} - d_{i,k}, \qquad k = 1, 2, \ldots, n_i - 1
$$

權重取**客戶內時間序號**（越晚發生權重越大）：

$$
w_{i,k} = k
$$

則：

$$
\mathrm{MLE}_i \;=\; \frac{1}{n_i-1}\sum_{k=1}^{n_i-1} g_{i,k}
\qquad\text{（Mean Length of Elapse：單純平均消費間隔）}
$$

$$
\mathrm{WMLE}_i \;=\; \frac{\sum_{k=1}^{n_i-1} g_{i,k}\cdot w_{i,k}}{\sum_{k=1}^{n_i-1} w_{i,k}}
\;=\; \frac{\sum_k \mathrm{Product}_{i,k}}{\sum_k \mathrm{Weight}_{i,k}}
\qquad\text{（Weighted MLE：近期加權平均間隔）}
$$

$$
\boxed{\;\mathrm{CAI}_i \;=\; \frac{\mathrm{MLE}_i - \mathrm{WMLE}_i}{\mathrm{MLE}_i}\times 100\;}
$$

#### 解讀規則

| CAI | 意義 | 數學條件 | 行銷動作 |
|-----|------|---------|---------|
| $> 0$ | 近期間隔**變短** → 消費**加速** | WMLE < MLE | 趁勢加碼：里程碑回饋、類別加速器 |
| $= 0$ | 節奏穩定 | WMLE = MLE | 維持關係，勿過度促銷 |
| $< 0$ | 近期間隔**變長** → 消費**減速**（靜默流失） | WMLE > MLE | 高價值挽回方案、個人化外撥 |

#### Excel 五步驟（原始公式，逐字保留）

| 步驟 | Sheet | 動作 | 原始 Excel 公式 |
|------|-------|------|----------------|
| 1 | `Step 1` | 建立「客戶×日」唯一鍵並去重 | `=A2&B2` |
| 2 | `Step 2` | 算距下次消費的天數，最後一列給哨兵 | `=IF(A2=A3, B3-B2, 9999)` |
| 3 | `Step 3` | 剔除 9999；建客戶內時間序號權重；相乘 | `=IF(A3=A2, D2+1, 1)` 與 `=C2*D2` |
| 4 | `Step 4` | 樞紐分析：依客戶取 `Average of int`、`Sum of Weight`、`Sum of Product` | （樞紐表，無公式） |
| 5 | `Step 5` | 算 WMLE 與 CAI | `=D2/C2` 與 `=((B2-E2)/B2)*100` |

#### 用純 SQL 重現（一段搞定，取代五個 Sheet）

```sql
WITH days AS (            -- Step 1: 客戶×日去重（同日多筆視為一次消費事件）
  SELECT DISTINCT 客戶ID AS cust, CAST(刷卡日期 AS DATE) AS d
  FROM transactions
),
gaps AS (                 -- Step 2: LEAD() 取代 =IF(A2=A3, B3-B2, 9999)；NULL 天然取代 9999 哨兵
  SELECT cust, d,
         date_diff('day', d, LEAD(d) OVER (PARTITION BY cust ORDER BY d)) AS gap
  FROM days
),
w AS (                    -- Step 3: 剔除 NULL；ROW_NUMBER() 就是 Weight；Product = gap × weight
  SELECT cust, d, gap,
         ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY cust ORDER BY d)       AS weight,
         gap * ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY cust ORDER BY d) AS product
  FROM gaps WHERE gap IS NOT NULL
),
agg AS (                  -- Step 4: 樞紐分析 → GROUP BY
  SELECT cust, avg(gap) AS mle, sum(weight) AS sum_w, sum(product) AS sum_p
  FROM w GROUP BY cust
)
SELECT cust, mle, sum_w, sum_p,                                  -- Step 5
       sum_p::DOUBLE / sum_w                        AS wmle,
       ((mle - (sum_p::DOUBLE/sum_w)) / mle) * 100  AS cai
FROM agg ORDER BY cai DESC;
```

21/99 ≈ 21.2%，接近但不等於五分位（20%）。界線恰落在 CAI ≈ **+11** 與 **−11**，近似對稱。
**若要在 Skill 中泛化，建議改為「CAI > +10 → Gradually Active；−10 ≤ CAI ≤ +10 → Steady；CAI < −10 → Gradually Unactive」**，
這樣不受樣本數變動影響，且與本檔結果幾乎一致。

**【材料原文】Summary 表附帶的三段文字洞察（原文照錄，英文）：**

> **Gradually Active**：
> "Although their total average spending (103,497) and transaction frequency are currently lower than the Steady segment, their positive CAI indicates that their purchase intervals are shrinking. These customers are in a "warm-up" phase, engaging with the card more frequently today than they have in the past. This group represents the highest ROI for targeted marketing; because they are already trending upward, "Momentum-Based Incentives" such as milestone spending bonuses or category-specific accelerators can effectively solidify their transition into the high-frequency Steady segment."

> **Steady**：
> "The "Steady" group represents the backbone of the portfolio, comprising approximately 58% of the total client base. With an average total spending of 192,136, these customers are the primary revenue drivers. Demographically, they skew toward married individuals with higher education levels, suggesting a lifestyle where credit card usage is deeply integrated into regular household or professional expenses. Strategic focus for this group should remain on "Relationship Maintenance" through high-tier loyalty rewards and exclusive service offerings that reinforce their current habits without the need for aggressive price-based incentives."

> **Gradually Unactive**：
> "While these clients may still hold significant historical value, their transaction intervals are stretching significantly beyond their historical averages, signaling a loss of "Top of Mind" status or a shift to a competitor's card. Unlike the other groups, these customers are in a state of "Silent Churn." Because their engagement is decelerating, traditional marketing is often insufficient. A "Re-engagement Strategy" is required here, utilizing high-value "Win-back" offers or personalized outreach to identify the friction points causing their departure before they become completely dormant."

**【評註】報表格式偏好（從此表可歸納）：**
1. 每個分群一列 + **必附 `Total (Benchmark)` 對照列**（讓讀者知道「相對於全體」是高是低）。
2. 數值欄一律用 `Average of X` / `Ratio` / `% of Y` 命名，**比率用 0–1 小數而非百分比字串**。
3. 人口統計欄一律轉為**單一比率**（% of Male、% of Bachelor or above、% of Married、% of Greater Taipei），
   而非展開成多欄計數 → **便於橫向比較**。
4. **右側緊接一欄 `Insights` 放整段英文敘述**，每段結構固定為：
   **現況數字 → 行為解釋 → 策略命名（加引號的專有名詞，如 "Momentum-Based Incentives"、"Silent Churn"、"Win-back"）→ 具體戰術。**
